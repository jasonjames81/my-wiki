## Honest summary of research-process limitations
- Several primary PDFs (WHO disability report, ILO WSPR, ILO migrant workers report, World Bank/Williams Institute Serbia LGBTI report, ECLAC CEPAL PDFs, St. Louis Fed blog) returned either HTTP 403 (bot-blocked) or unreadable binary/encoded content when fetched directly. Where this happened, the reported stat was instead drawn from the search tool's own extraction of that same document's text (visible in the raw search results), or from independent secondary sources directly quoting/citing the primary report by name, author, and year. This is flagged per-row above; it is weaker than a direct, manually re-read primary-source confirmation, but stronger than an uncorroborated claim — none of the stats above were invented or extrapolated.
- Item 1 (caste × gender) and item 8 (religion) did NOT yield a strong current quantitative citation despite multiple search passes; both are flagged as gaps rather than papered over.
