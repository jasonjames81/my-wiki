This site is a personal research portfolio. Each entry takes a hard question, surveys the best available evidence, and lays out what it does and does not support.

## Authorship and accountability

The author, Jason James, is responsible for all content on this site — its claims, framing, conclusions, and any errors. Nothing here is published without human review.

## How it was produced

The research was produced with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) (Anthropic's AI agent tool, built on the Claude family of models). The model was used to gather and organize sources, draft and structure prose, and check internal consistency. It is a tool in the workflow, not an author: it carries no accountability for the content, is not listed as an author, and is not cited as a source.

## Verification

Every factual claim is tied to a primary or peer-reviewed source, cited inline. Citations were **independently verified** — confirming that each source exists, says what it is cited as saying, and resolves to a working link — rather than taken on the model's word. Where the evidence is genuinely contested, the text says so and presents the disagreement.

## Sources and citations

Citations point to the underlying scholarship — journals, working papers, datasets, and books — not to the AI tool. External links open in a new tab. Disclosing the model here, as part of the method, follows emerging norms for LLM-assisted research: the tool gets a methods disclosure, the sources get the citations.

## Privacy and data

The research draws only on public sources. No personal, confidential, or copyrighted full-text material is fed into the tooling.
