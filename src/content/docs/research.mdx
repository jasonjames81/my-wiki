This research focuses on interventions with **at least one published empirical evaluation** or strong historical precedent. Speculative or untested proposals are noted but flagged. Geographic scope is global, with weight given to interventions feasible in high-income democracies and at international institutions, because that is where the largest stocks of concentrated wealth currently sit.

The synthesis is opinionated: it ranks interventions by a combination of effect size, political tractability, and reversibility. Reasonable people will weight these differently — readers are encouraged to follow the citations and form their own judgment.
